Overview

A Trusted Aftercare Resource for the Families You Serve

After a death, families often complete funeral arrangements and then realize they still have practical questions about paperwork, accounts, property, estate responsibilities, and whether probate applies. Funeral directors do not need to answer those questions. They need a safe, professional place to point families when those questions come up.

Probatem is not asking funeral homes to sell software. Probatem gives funeral homes a trusted aftercare resource they can recommend when families ask, "What do we do next?"
